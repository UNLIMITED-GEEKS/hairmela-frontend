Account

Delete your account

You can ask us to delete your HairMela account and the data associated with it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. 1

    Tap the button below, or email us at {{ supportEmail }} from the email address on your account.

  2. 2

    Include your account email, and your salon name if you own or work at one, so we can find the right account.

  3. 3

    We will confirm the request by replying to that address, then delete the account within 30 days.

Request account deletion

No email app? Use the contact form instead and mention that you want your account deleted.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, name, email address, and phone number.
  • Your queue history and any appointments you booked.
  • Your notification settings and any devices registered for push.
  • If you own a salon: the salon, its staff, services, and photos.

What we may keep

We keep records we are legally required to retain, such as payment and invoice records for completed subscriptions. These are kept only as long as the law requires and are not used to contact you.

Before you request deletion

  • Deletion is permanent. Your account cannot be restored afterwards.
  • If you own a salon, deleting your account also removes it for your staff and customers.
  • If you have an active subscription, cancel it first so you are not billed again.

For more on how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy.
