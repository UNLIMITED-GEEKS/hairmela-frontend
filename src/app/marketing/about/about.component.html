About us

We turn your queue into a number your clients can watch.

HairMela helps barbershops, salons, spas, makeup studios, and independent beauty professionals manage walk-ins and appointments in one place. Clients can join the queue from their phone, follow their position in real time, or book ahead and arrive when it is nearly their turn.

Our story

Anyone who has waited at a busy barbershop, salon, spa, or studio knows the feeling: you do not know whether you are next or still have an hour to wait. Some clients leave, while the team spends the day answering the same question: “How long will it take?”

We built HairMela to make that easier. Clients can check their position and estimated wait time from their phone or book ahead for another day. The people serving them can manage the queue just as easily, without buying extra equipment.
