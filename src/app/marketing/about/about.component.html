About us

We turn the salon queue into a number you can watch.

HairMela is a walk-in queue built for barbershops and salons — so customers join the line from their phone, see their position update live, and walk in right when it’s their turn.

Our story

Anyone who’s sat in a crowded barbershop knows the feeling: you don’t know if you’re next or if you’ve got an hour to kill. The shop loses walk-ins who give up at the door, and the barber loses focus answering “how long?” all day.

HairMela started from that simple frustration. We wanted a tool that gives the customer a live position and an honest wait, and gives the barber a calm, one-tap way to run the line — all from a phone, with no hardware to buy.
