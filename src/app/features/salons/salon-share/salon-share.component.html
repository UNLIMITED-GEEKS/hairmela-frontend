Want faster access next time? Install HairMela to your device — no app store needed.

Android

  1. 1 Open this page in Chrome (or your browser of choice).
  2. 2 Tap the ⋮ menu button in the top-right corner.
  3. 3 Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. 4 Confirm — HairMela's icon appears on your home screen and app drawer.

iPhone & iPad

Only works in Safari — Chrome and other browsers can't install apps on iPhone/iPad.

  1. 1 Open this page in Safari.
  2. 2 Tap the Share icon (a square with an arrow pointing up) in the toolbar.
  3. 3 Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. (If you don't see it, tap Edit Actions at the bottom of the list and add it.)
  4. 4 Tap Add in the top-right corner — HairMela's icon appears on your home screen.
